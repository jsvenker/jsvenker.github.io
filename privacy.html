Privacy policy

This OAuth app is a personal tool. Its only user is Sam Venker, who uses it so that his own AI assistant can read and manage data in his own Google account (Gmail drafts, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks and Contacts) on his request.

The data is processed only on computers Sam controls and on the AI service he uses to run the assistant. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
